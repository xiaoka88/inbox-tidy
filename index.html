Inbox Tidy

A personal, single-user mail-cleanup tool

Inbox Tidy is a private helper that runs on my own computer and cleans up my own mailbox. It sorts bulk mail into tiers, shows me a read-only report first, and only moves messages to the trash after I explicitly confirm. Nothing is ever deleted permanently — everything can be recovered from the trash for 30 days.

What it does, step by step
  • Scan (read-only). Reads message headers and a short text preview to group mail by sender and category. No changes are made.
  • Report. Prints counts per group plus examples, so I can see exactly what would be affected before anything happens.
  • Confirm. Nothing is written until I approve the specific list.
  • Act. Moves the approved messages to Gmail's trash, or applies a label, using the Gmail API. Messages stay in the trash for 30 days.

Why it needs Google account access

To read and organise my mailbox the tool needs my permission to use the Gmail API. It requests read, compose, and modify access so that it can list messages, read their headers, apply labels, and move messages to the trash. It can also read calendars and files, because the same helper keeps small personal notes and schedules on the side.

Privacy

This is a single-user tool with exactly one user: me. It does not have accounts, sign-ups, or other users. Mail content never leaves my own machine, except as requests sent directly to Google's own APIs. Details are in the Privacy Policy, and the terms of use are in the Terms of Service.
